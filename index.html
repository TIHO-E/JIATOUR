BEFORE YOU GO

A little planning
A smoother journey

What information do you need for a quote?

Your travel date, passenger numbers, pickup and destination are a good start. Include any return journey, extra stops, luggage or specific access requirements so we can discuss a suitable option.

Which vehicle should I choose?

We list 7–14, 24, 34 and 57-seat options. Passenger numbers are only part of the picture: luggage and your itinerary matter too. Choose “Help me choose” on the form if you’re unsure.

Do you travel outside Melbourne?

Yes. Jia Tour provides charter transport throughout Victoria and across Australia. Send us your proposed itinerary to discuss availability and a quote.

Does sending an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. The form requests a quote. Your vehicle, price and booking arrangements need to be confirmed directly with Jia Tour.
