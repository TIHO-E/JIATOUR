YOUR ENQUIRY

Test enquiry privacy.

This notice applies to the new enquiry test page. The main website continues to use FormSubmit until the new form is activated. For questions about your information, contact Jia Tour Pty Ltd at jiatour@hotmail.com or 0430 218 539.

What the form collects

The form asks for your name, email address, phone number and trip details, such as passenger numbers, travel date, pickup, destination and vehicle preference. Include only information needed to discuss your journey. Please do not send payment card details, identification documents or medical records through this form.

Where your enquiry goes

When you submit, Cloudflare Workers processes your enquiry and Resend delivers it to Jia Tour’s enquiry email address. Cloudflare Turnstile performs a security check to help prevent automated abuse. Our website code does not save form entries to a database or browser storage; the service providers process submitted information and technical request data as part of providing their services. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy, Turnstile’s privacy information and Resend’s privacy policy.

How the information is used

Your enquiry details allow Jia Tour to contact you and discuss your requested transport and quote. Resend sends an automatic acknowledgement with a summary of your journey to the email address you provide. This acknowledgement does not confirm a quotation or booking. Resend states that account data, including email metadata, logs and API records, is stored in the United States; choosing its Tokyo sending region does not change that storage location. If you need to correct information you have sent or ask about how it is handled, please contact Jia Tour using the details above.

Promotional links

If you arrive through a promotional link, the internal notification to Jia Tour includes the campaign source, medium, name and content label from that link. These labels are excluded from your acknowledgement. These labels help identify where an enquiry came from. This website does not store form entries or campaign labels in cookies or browser storage.

This version of the website does not load advertising pixels or analytics tags. The hosting and form providers may process technical request information as part of providing their services.
