Give each stop enough detail

List your proposed destinations in order, together with any attraction entry, restaurant booking or activity start times. Include how long you plan to stay at each stop and the time you would like to return. If the itinerary is still taking shape, identify the confirmed parts and the ideas you would like to discuss.

Keep transport and activity bookings clear

A charter enquiry is a request for group transport. Ask Jia Tour to explain the quote inclusions and confirm whether any other arrangements have been agreed. Attraction tickets, tastings, meals and guided activities should not be assumed to be included. Sharing your existing bookings helps the team understand the timing requirements.

Choose a vehicle for the whole day

Jia Tour lists 7–14, 24, 34 and 57-seat options. Share the total passenger count, pickup addresses, luggage or equipment and any access needs. Let us know if people will join or leave during the day. Vehicle suitability and availability are confirmed with the team as part of discussing your journey.

BEFORE YOU GO

Good to know

A few details to help you plan.

Can we suggest our own day-trip itinerary?

Yes. Share your proposed destinations, timings and passenger numbers. Jia Tour will review the transport request and discuss availability and a quote.

Are attraction tickets or tastings included?

Do not assume they are included in charter transport. Confirm the quote inclusions directly with Jia Tour and identify any activity bookings you have already made.

Can I enquire before the itinerary is final?

Yes. Include the details you know and explain which dates, stops or timings are still flexible. The team can discuss the next information needed for your quote.
