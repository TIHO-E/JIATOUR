Send the whole route

For an interstate journey, include the departure point, final destination and each planned stop in between. Explain whether the trip is one way or returns to Melbourne. Dates and locations for overnight stays help the team understand where the group and vehicle will need to be on each day.

Separate travel time from activities

List any fixed appointments, attraction entry times or events, alongside the time you would like to spend at each stop. Tell us which parts of the schedule are flexible. Jia Tour can then discuss the proposed transport arrangements with you before the itinerary and booking are confirmed.

Plan for a longer journey

Passenger numbers, luggage and equipment all matter when considering a vehicle for an extended trip. The listed fleet options are 7–14, 24, 34 and 57 seats. Describe access needs and any changes to the group during the journey. Ask for the quote inclusions to be explained, including any arrangements connected with overnight travel; do not assume accommodation, meals or attraction bookings are part of the charter.

BEFORE YOU GO

Good to know

A few details to help you plan.

Can I request a one-way interstate charter?

Include the departure point, final destination and one-way requirement in your enquiry. Jia Tour will review the route and dates and discuss availability and a quote.

What details are needed for a multi-day trip?

Send the daily route, overnight locations, travel dates, passenger count and any fixed event times. Include luggage and access requirements so the full request can be considered.

Does the quote include hotels and attractions?

This website requests charter transport. Ask Jia Tour to specify all inclusions and exclusions; do not assume hotels, meals or attraction bookings are included.
