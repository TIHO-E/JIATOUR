Connect the important parts of the day

For a wedding, list the accommodation, ceremony and reception locations and explain which parts of the day require transport. Tell us when guests must arrive and when each event ends. If you would like different return times for separate groups, include those details in the initial enquiry so the proposed journeys can be reviewed together.

Plan for your actual guest list

Count every passenger and identify who is travelling on each leg. A guest total for the whole event may differ from the number needing transport. The listed 7–14, 24, 34 and 57-seat options offer a starting point; luggage, access requirements and availability also affect which option can be arranged.

Make collection easy to understand

Provide full venue addresses and any access or parking instructions supplied by the venue. Include a nominated organiser’s contact details so plans can be discussed in one place. The same information helps with birthday celebrations, family outings and club gatherings, whether the group needs a one-way journey or a return trip.

BEFORE YOU GO

Good to know

A few details to help you plan.

Can I enquire about different guest return times?

Yes. Include each proposed return time, destination and passenger count. Jia Tour will review the request and discuss what can be arranged.

Can you quote for a family or club outing?

Yes. Choose Private group & wedding on the enquiry form and describe the outing, date, group size and route. The team will discuss availability and a quote.

Are venue access arrangements included automatically?

Please share any access, parking or collection instructions from your venue. Confirm the final meeting points and transport arrangements with Jia Tour before travel.
