ROOM FOR YOUR GROUP

7–57 seat options

Passenger numbers, luggage and your itinerary help us find the right fit.

Explore our fleet

For arriving groups

Include the flight number, arrival date and scheduled arrival time, plus the airport and terminal. Explain whether the whole group arrives on one flight or separately. The landing time and the time passengers are ready for collection are different parts of the plan; agree on the meeting point and pickup arrangements with Jia Tour before travelling.

For departing groups

Send the pickup address, flight departure details and the time your group needs to reach the terminal. Tell us about additional collection stops and any passengers joining along the way. Your enquiry should identify the organiser or travelling contact who can coordinate the group on the day.

Match luggage as well as seats

Jia Tour lists 7–14, 24, 34 and 57-seat options. Include the number and approximate size of suitcases, along with oversized items such as sports equipment. A vehicle’s listed seats do not confirm that every luggage combination will fit. Ask the team to confirm capacity and access arrangements for your particular group.

BEFORE YOU GO

Good to know

A few details to help you plan.

Does Jia Tour provide individual scheduled airport tickets?

This enquiry service is for group charter transport. Send the details of your own group to request a quote; the website does not sell individual scheduled shuttle tickets.

What should I tell you about luggage?

Include the quantity and approximate size of suitcases and any oversized items. Jia Tour needs these details to discuss vehicle suitability as well as passenger capacity.

What happens if our flight details change?

Contact Jia Tour with the updated flight information as soon as possible. Confirm any revised pickup time, meeting point and arrangements directly with the team.
