Plan around your school day

An excursion itinerary needs more than a destination. Include the time your group must arrive, the time you expect to leave and when students need to be back at school. If you have several visits in one day, list each stop in order. Tell us whether the vehicle is needed between venues or only for the outward and return journeys.

Count everyone travelling

Include teachers, supervising adults and other accompanying passengers in your total. Jia Tour lists 7–14, 24, 34 and 57-seat options. The right choice also depends on bags, equipment, access needs and vehicle availability. If your group is larger than one listed vehicle, include the full group size in your enquiry so arrangements can be discussed.

Make pickup arrangements clear

Provide the school name, street address, preferred entrance and a contact for the organiser. Mention known restrictions around gates, bus bays or the destination entrance. Ask the team about any documents your school needs before confirming the booking, and share any required arrival or collection procedure.

BEFORE YOU GO

Good to know

A few details to help you plan.

Should teachers be included in the passenger count?

Yes. Include every person travelling, including teachers, supervisors and other accompanying adults, when requesting your quote.

Can I enquire about more than one excursion stop?

Yes. List the venues in order with the time needed at each location. Jia Tour will review the proposed route and discuss availability and the transport arrangements.

Is the excursion booked when I send the form?

No. The form starts an enquiry. Confirm the vehicle, itinerary, price and booking arrangements directly with Jia Tour before treating transport as booked.
