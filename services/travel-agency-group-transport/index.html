Bring the itinerary into one brief

Include airport movements, hotel addresses, daily visits and any interstate travel in the same request. Mark the travel dates and required arrival times for each leg. If arrangements are provisional, identify what is confirmed and what may change, rather than assuming a tentative itinerary reserves a vehicle.

Describe the travelling group

Include passengers, tour leaders and guides in the total. Provide luggage quantities, equipment and access requirements, along with any change in group size between legs. Jia Tour lists 7–14, 24, 34 and 57-seat options; the team will discuss suitability and availability for the itinerary you submit.

Agree on contacts and responsibilities

Nominate an agency contact for the quote and an on-trip contact for the group. Share relevant collection instructions from hotels, airports and venues. Ask Jia Tour to set out the transport inclusions, exclusions and confirmation requirements. Guiding, accommodation, attraction tickets and other travel arrangements should be clarified separately rather than assumed to form part of a charter enquiry.

BEFORE YOU GO

Good to know

A few details to help you plan.

Should guides and tour leaders be counted as passengers?

Yes. Include everyone who needs a seat, including guides and tour leaders, and explain any passenger changes between transport legs.

Can one enquiry cover airport transfers and touring?

Yes. Send the complete itinerary with the dates, locations and passenger numbers for each leg so Jia Tour can review the combined transport request.

Does an enquiry reserve the requested vehicle?

No. Availability, vehicle choice, pricing and booking arrangements must be confirmed directly with Jia Tour. An automatic acknowledgement only confirms receipt of the enquiry.
