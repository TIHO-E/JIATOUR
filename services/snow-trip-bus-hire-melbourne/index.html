Start with the destination and timing

Name the snowfield or resort, your preferred travel date and whether you want a day trip or a longer stay. Include your Melbourne pickup address, intended arrival time and planned departure from the mountain. Share any activities or accommodation that affect the timetable, so the transport request can be reviewed as a complete itinerary.

Allow for winter equipment

Tell us how many people are travelling and whether the group will carry skis, snowboards, boots, bags or other bulky equipment. Jia Tour lists 7–14, 24, 34 and 57-seat options, but the available space and suitability for a particular alpine journey must be discussed separately. Please describe access requirements at the time of enquiry.

Confirm the details before booking activities

Ask the team to clarify the proposed drop-off and collection points, what is included in the transport quote and how changes to the itinerary will be handled. Do not assume resort entry, lift passes, equipment hire or accommodation are included in a charter enquiry. Vehicle availability and final travel arrangements must be confirmed directly with Jia Tour.

BEFORE YOU GO

Good to know

A few details to help you plan.

Are lift passes or equipment hire included?

A website enquiry requests transport. Do not assume lift passes, resort entry, equipment hire or accommodation are included; ask Jia Tour to specify the inclusions in your quote.

Can we bring skis and snowboards?

Include the quantities and sizes of winter equipment when you enquire. Jia Tour must confirm space and vehicle suitability for your proposed journey.

Can I enquire about an overnight snow trip?

Yes. Send the full dates, destination, accommodation location and proposed daily transport needs. The team will review the request and discuss availability.
