YOUR ENQUIRY

Enquiry privacy.

This notice explains how the enquiry form on this website works. For questions about your information, contact Jia Tour Pty Ltd at jiatour@hotmail.com or 0430 218 539.

What the form collects

The form asks for your name, email address, phone number and trip details, such as passenger numbers, travel date, pickup, destination and vehicle preference. Include only information needed to discuss your journey. Please do not send payment card details, identification documents or medical records through this form.

Where your enquiry goes

When you submit, your information is sent to FormSubmit, the third-party form service, for processing and forwarding to Jia Tour’s enquiry email address. FormSubmit may present a security check. See FormSubmit’s privacy information for its handling of submitted information.

How the information is used

Your enquiry details allow Jia Tour to contact you and discuss your requested transport and quote. FormSubmit also sends an automatic acknowledgement and a copy of your enquiry to the email address you provide. If you need to correct information you have sent or ask about how it is handled, please contact Jia Tour using the details above.

Promotional links

If you arrive through a promotional link, the form includes the campaign source, medium, name and content label from that link. These labels help identify where an enquiry came from. This website does not store form entries or campaign labels in cookies or browser storage.

This version of the website does not load advertising pixels or analytics tags. The hosting and form providers may process technical request information as part of providing their services.
